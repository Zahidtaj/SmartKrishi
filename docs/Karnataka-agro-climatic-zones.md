# Karnataka Agro-Climatic Zones: Detailed Regional Compendium
## Official Delineation by Department of Agriculture & Planning Commission

Karnataka possesses one of the most ecologically diverse agricultural landscapes in India, spanning arid scrublands, black cotton plains, red loamy plateaus, western ghat rainforests, and tropical coastal alluviums.

---
